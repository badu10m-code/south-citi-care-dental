Services

Every dental problem has a fix. We find yours.

Each visit starts with a proper diagnosis and a clear explanation of your options. No upselling, no surprises on the bill.

Most asked for · usually one visit

Root Canal (Single Sitting)

Infected or badly decayed teeth are cleaned, filled and sealed, most often in one visit under local anaesthesia. You keep your natural tooth and walk out pain-free.

Permanent fix · certified implantologist

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed by a certified implantologist, topped with a natural-looking crown. The closest thing to growing a new tooth, built to last decades.

Cosmetic · plan first, treat after

Teeth Whitening & Smile Design

Professional whitening, veneers and complete smile makeovers. We plan the result before we touch a single tooth.

Everything else we treat

  • Crowns & Bridges

    Custom caps and bridges by an M.D.S prosthodontist, matched to your neighbouring teeth so closely that only you will know.

  • Fillings & Gum Treatment

    Tooth-coloured fillings that disappear into your smile, deep cleaning, and gum surgery where needed. Bleeding gums are a problem to fix, never a thing to ignore.

  • Extractions & Family Care

    From wisdom teeth to a child's first filling, done gently and calmly. Nervous patients are our speciality: tell us and we go at your pace.

  • Laser Dentistry

    Laser treatments for gum reshaping and cosmetic work: less bleeding, faster healing, fewer visits.

  • Braces & Aligners

    Straightening for teens and adults, with metal braces and clear aligner options discussed openly with costs.

  • Dental Emergencies

    Broken tooth, severe pain or swelling? Call us and we fit you in the same day whenever possible.

Not sure which treatment you need?

Book a check-up. Diagnosis and explanation come first; treatment only after you say yes.
