What we treat

Nine specialities, one clinic in New Alipore

Every service below is priced after the examination, in writing, before anything starts. If a tooth can be saved without a procedure, that is the plan you will hear first.

01 · Endodontics

Root Canal (Single Sitting)

When decay or a crack reaches the nerve inside a tooth, the infection does not settle on its own. A root canal removes the infected tissue, disinfects the space inside the roots and seals it, so the tooth stays in your mouth and keeps doing its job.

Front teeth and straightforward cases are usually finished in one visit of 60 to 90 minutes. Molars with curved roots, or teeth with active infection and swelling, are safer over two or three visits so the disinfection can work properly between appointments. Either way, the plan is decided after an X-ray and told to you plainly.

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, and the numbness is checked before any work starts. Patients describe pressure rather than pain.

02 · Implantology

Dental Implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone, the bone grows onto it over a few months, and a crown is fixed on top. It stands on its own and asks nothing of the neighbouring teeth.

The planning matters more than the hardware. Dr. Anish Bharti holds a certificate in dental implantology and does the assessment, the placement and the final crown in one clinic, so nothing is outsourced halfway. Bone quality, gum health and the bite above the gap are all checked before a date is set.

03 · Prosthodontics

Crowns & Bridges

A crown caps a damaged or root-canal-treated tooth; a bridge spans a gap using the teeth on either side for support. Both are made from zirconia or ceramic, shaded to match the teeth beside them.

After a root canal, a crown is usually what stands between the treated tooth and a fracture years later, so it is planned as part of the same treatment rather than sold as an afterthought.

04 · Orthodontics

Braces & Aligners

Metal braces, ceramic braces and clear aligners all move teeth; they differ in visibility, cost and how much discipline they demand from you. The right one depends on the case, the timeline and honestly, on what you will actually keep up with.

Every orthodontic plan here comes with the timeline in writing: how long, how many reviews, and what the total comes to.

05 · Paediatric care

Kids' Dentistry

A child's first visit should end with them wanting to come back. Ours are kept short and gentle: a look, a count of the teeth, and a first sit in the chair. Nothing invasive on day one.

From there, fluoride applications, fillings for milk teeth, and habit counseling for thumb-sucking or night grinding, all explained to the child in words they understand before anything is done.

06 · Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening & Smile Design

In-clinic whitening lightens teeth by several shades in one sitting, with the shade recorded before and after so the change is measurable, not a matter of opinion.

Smile design covers reshaping, veneers and spacing corrections. It starts from your own teeth and your own face, not from a template. Some stains are internal and will not respond to whitening at all; if yours are that kind, you will be told so before you spend anything.

07 · Restorative

Fillings & Gum Treatment

Tooth-coloured composite fillings for new cavities, and replacement of old, leaking ones before they turn into root canals. Scaling and polishing removes the hardened plaque that brushing cannot.

Gum treatment is where a lot of long-term damage is quietly prevented. Bleeding gums are a sign, not a phase, and early gum disease is far easier to arrest than advanced gum disease.

08 · Technology

Laser Dentistry

Where the case suits it, laser dentistry replaces the scalpel for gum procedures: less bleeding, fewer sutures and easier healing afterwards.

It is offered for gum contouring, frenectomies and gum disease treatment. If a case is better served by a conventional approach, you will be told that, and why.

09 · Same-day

Dental Emergencies

Swelling, a broken tooth, a knocked-out tooth or pain that will not let you sleep: same-day slots are kept aside for exactly these. Call or WhatsApp +91 99034 50487 and describe the problem; you will be told whether to come in straight away.

A knocked-out adult tooth has the best odds if it reaches a dentist within an hour. Keep it in milk or inside the cheek, do not scrub the root, and come.
