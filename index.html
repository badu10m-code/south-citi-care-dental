Common questions

Before you call

Do root canals hurt?

Every root canal is done under local anaesthesia. Patients describe pressure rather than pain, and most single-sitting cases finish within 60 to 90 minutes. The phrase that appears most often in our Google reviews is "completely painless".

How much does treatment cost?

The full figure is quoted after the examination and X-ray, before any treatment starts. It covers the procedure and the crown or follow-up visits it needs, and it does not change mid-treatment.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Children's first visits are kept short and gentle: a look, a count of the teeth, and a first sit in the chair. Nothing invasive on day one.

Where exactly are you?

23B/40/1A, near PNB, Mani Aangan, Block B, New Alipore, Kolkata 700053, next to the Punjab National Bank branch.

Do you take walk-ins?

Booked patients are seen on schedule, and same-day slots are kept aside for emergencies: swelling, broken teeth, severe pain. Calling or messaging first is still the faster route.
