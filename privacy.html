Privacy & Security

A dental clinic handles two kinds of trust: the kind in the chair, and the kind on paper. This page is about the second.

What this website collects

When you fill the appointment form, the website receives only what you typed into it: your name, your phone number, the treatment you are asking about, your preferred date and time, and any note you chose to add. Nothing else. There is no account to create, no social login, and the website never asks for contact-list access.

What the website never does

  • It does not sell, share or pass your details to any third party for marketing. Ever.
  • It does not run third-party advertising trackers or pop-up scripts.
  • It does not ask for health records, ID numbers or payment details through the website.
  • It never uploads your phone's contacts or "finds friends".

How appointment requests travel

The site is served over HTTPS, so what you type is encrypted in transit. A submitted request goes to the clinic owner's private dashboard, and from there the clinic confirms your slot by WhatsApp or a phone call. If you would rather be called instead of messaged, say so in the note field.

Clinic records are separate

Treatment records, X-rays and case history live inside the clinic's own records, not on this website. The website team does not see them. Clinical information shared in the chair stays between you and your dentist, subject to a patient's consent, the same as at any dental practice.

Security on the owner's side

The admin dashboard that receives appointment requests is accessible only to the clinic owner. On the live site it sits behind the owner's own login. Appointment data is stored encrypted and purged on request; a patient can ask the clinic to delete their details at any time by phone or WhatsApp.

Questions about your data

Call +91 99034 50487 or message the clinic on WhatsApp. Ask for the owner, and any record connected to you can be shown, corrected or removed.

In short: the website takes your name and number for one purpose, confirming your appointment. It uses them for nothing else and gives them to no one.
